Custom software development in Ohio

Business apps, websites and online stores, built in Ohio, priced like a subscription, explained in plain English.

Stackpine is an Ohio custom software company. We build the tools small businesses actually run on (intake systems, booking tools, dashboards, online stores) for a monthly subscription instead of a five-figure upfront bill. Based here, serving businesses everywhere.

Why Ohio businesses work with us

  • Same-day replies, same time zone. Message us in the morning, hear back before lunch, not a ticket queue in another hemisphere.
  • Plain English, no jargon. You describe how your shop, practice or crew actually works. We translate that into software. The translation is our job, not yours.
  • Subscription pricing. No $40,000 agency quotes. One affordable monthly fee covers the build, hosting, security, support and ongoing improvements. Here’s how the cost model works.
  • One ongoing relationship. The person who builds your software is the person who answers when you need a change made.

What we build

A few recent builds for real organizations, with details kept anonymous, because our clients’ operations are their business:

  • A leadership dashboard for a healthcare organization that replaced a dozen weekly spreadsheets with one live view of programs, staffing and finances
  • A custom online store for an off-road parts retailer: inventory, checkout and an admin panel one person can run, without big-platform fees
  • An expense tracker for a small motorsports business: receipts snapped from a phone, totals tax-ready all year

Other common builds: request intake and triage boards, online booking and scheduling, customer portals, quoting and inventory tools, giveaway and promotion tools, and custom websites and storefronts that we host and maintain for you.

Where we work across Ohio

Everything we build runs on the web, so the work itself isn’t bound by geography, but being in the same state and the same time zone changes how a project feels. You get same-day replies, and “let’s hop on a quick call” actually happens.

  • Cleveland and Northeast Ohio. Manufacturing, healthcare, trades and motorsports businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets but have no interest in a five-figure agency engagement.
  • Columbus and Central Ohio. Fast-growing service businesses and nonprofits that need internal tools built faster than a traditional dev shop can scope them.
  • Cincinnati and Southwest Ohio. Retail and e-commerce operations looking to escape per-transaction platform fees with a custom storefront.
  • Akron, Canton, Toledo, Dayton and the Ohio Valley. Smaller operations where one person wears five hats and custom business software removes three of them.

What custom software costs for an Ohio small business

The honest answer is that it costs far less than the quotes that scared you off. Ohio agencies routinely quote $20,000 to $100,000 up front for custom business applications, then hand over the finished product and move on. That model prices out exactly the businesses that benefit most.

We work on a monthly subscription instead. There’s no large upfront build cost, and the fee covers hosting, security, support, updates and ongoing improvements. For most small businesses, the monthly figure lands near what they already spend on the three or four almost-right apps it replaces. Every project is quoted individually and the quote is free.
