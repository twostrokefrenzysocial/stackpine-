Custom software for small businesses in Cleveland, OH

For Northeast Ohio shops, trades, practices and family businesses that run on paper, old spreadsheets and the one person who knows how it all works.

Cleveland and Northeast Ohio have a business community built over generations. Manufacturers and machine shops, a nationally known healthcare sector, contractors and trades, family-owned retailers, and a strong motorsports and powersports scene. Many of these businesses are on their second or third generation of owners, and many run on processes that work, but live on paper, in a spreadsheet nobody else understands, or in one long-time employee’s head.

Stackpine builds custom business software for exactly those businesses: quoting and inventory tools, intake systems, dashboards, customer portals and websites, shaped around how your shop or practice already runs. No five-figure agency bill up front. One monthly subscription covers the build, hosting, security, support and every improvement after launch.

When “the way we’ve always done it” starts costing you

Established businesses rarely need to change what they do. They need to stop losing time to how they track it. The signs tend to look like this:

  • Job tickets and quotes on paper, retyped into a spreadsheet, then retyped again into an invoice.
  • A spreadsheet only one person understands, and the whole operation slows down when they are out.
  • Knowledge that walks out the door when a long-time employee retires, because it was never written down anywhere useful.
  • Five subscriptions that each do 60% of the job, none of which talk to each other.

The fix is almost never a giant system. It is one tool that removes the worst of the retyping, in use within weeks, then grows from there. Read our plain-English guide to custom software for how to tell whether you need it.

How a Cleveland project starts

You describe how work moves through your business today, the way you would explain it to a new hire: where a job comes in, who touches it, where it gets written down, and where it tends to get stuck. You do not need a feature list or any technical knowledge. From that conversation we suggest the one tool that would save the most time, and give you a straight, free quote for it.

Most first versions are working within a few weeks, and you see progress the whole way through. After launch, the person who built it is the person who answers when you need a change, and the software keeps growing with your business, one layer at a time. If a well-known off-the-shelf product would do the job, we will point you at it instead.
