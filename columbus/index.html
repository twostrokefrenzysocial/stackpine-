For Columbus and Central Ohio businesses that are growing faster than their spreadsheets. Built around how you work, on one monthly fee.

Stackpine builds the custom software those businesses run on: intake and booking tools, customer portals, dashboards, websites and AI assistants, shaped around how your business actually works. When the business grows faster than the tools

Growth is the most common reason a Columbus owner starts looking at custom software. The tools that were fine at the start begin to crack in predictable places:

  • New hires keep asking the same questions. The answers exist in a handbook or a shared drive, but it is faster to ask whoever has been there longest, so the most experienced people spend their day being interrupted.
  • Requests arrive through too many doors. Phone, text, email, a web form and the occasional sticky note. At five people everyone remembers. At twenty, things go missing.
  • Scheduling turns into a daily puzzle. More staff, more customers and more locations, all coordinated by one person with a calendar and a lot of patience.
  • Nobody can see the whole picture. The numbers that tell you how the business is doing live in four places, and pulling them together is somebody’s Friday afternoon.

None of these need a huge system. They need one tool that fixes the most painful crack first, then grows one layer at a time as the business does. That is the whole idea behind how we work.
