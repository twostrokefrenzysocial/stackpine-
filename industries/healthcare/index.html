Custom software for healthcare organizations

Live reporting, staff assistants and privacy-first AI, built around how your organization already works.

Healthcare organizations run on information: programs, staffing, schedules, finances and policies. Too often that information is spread across a dozen spreadsheets and a shared drive, and the people who need it most are the ones spending hours each week pulling it together. We have built for healthcare organizations, and the most common first project is the simplest one to describe: one place where leadership can see what is going on.

Where healthcare organizations lose time

  • Weekly reporting by hand. Program numbers collected from separate spreadsheets, reconciled and retyped before anyone can act on them.
  • The same questions, every day. Policy and procedure answers that already exist in documents, but are faster to ask a supervisor for.
  • Data that cannot go to the cloud. Sensitive workflows where off-the-shelf AI tools are off the table.

What we build for healthcare

Secure dashboards and reporting that pull figures together automatically. Internal AI assistants that answer staff questions from your own documents and say so honestly when the documents do not cover something. Intake and triage tools so requests stop getting lost between channels. And, where data genuinely has to stay in the building, AI that runs on your own hardware. See everything we build.

A note on privacy

Our results are anonymous by design: how a healthcare organization staffs its programs is not ours to publish, and the same discretion applies to your build. Tell us what your privacy and compliance requirements are at the start, and we will shape the software, and where it runs, around them.
