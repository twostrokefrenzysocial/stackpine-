Industries we build for

Wildly different businesses, the same pattern: information arriving through too many channels and someone retyping it every week.

We have built for healthcare organizations, retail and e-commerce, motorsports and powersports, and service businesses, including a funeral service. Each page below covers the problems that come up in that kind of business, what we build for it, and the real, anonymous builds behind it.

Healthcare →Live reporting, staff assistants and privacy-first AI, built around how your organization already works. Retail & e-commerce →An online store that is actually yours, plus the inventory and admin tools behind it, without the platform squeeze. Motorsports & powersports →From parts stores to motorsports businesses to service shops, tools built around how your business actually runs. Service businesses →Requests, bookings, dispatch and follow-up in one place, so nothing falls through the cracks between the phone, the texts and the inbox. Funeral services →Structured case intake and assignment, built around how your service actually works, so no call or handoff gets missed.
