Custom software for retail and e-commerce

An online store that is actually yours, plus the inventory and admin tools behind it, without the platform squeeze.

Small retailers are squeezed from two sides. The big e-commerce platforms charge a monthly platform fee, app fees on top and a cut of every transaction, and the tools that are supposed to run the back office each do part of the job. We build custom storefronts and the retail tools behind them, then host and maintain them for one predictable monthly fee.

What we build for retailers

  • Custom online stores with product pages, checkout and an admin panel simple enough for one person to run.
  • Inventory and quoting so you know what you have, what it costs and what to charge.
  • Customer portals where buyers check an order’s status instead of calling.
  • Event and promo tools such as contests, drawings and giveaways your customers actually enjoy.

Escaping per-transaction fees

A platform that takes a cut of every sale grows its bill as your business grows. A custom storefront on a flat monthly subscription does not. One of our recent builds replaced a big-platform store for an off-road parts retailer with full inventory, checkout and order management, run day to day by a single owner. Read how our websites and online stores work.

When an off-the-shelf platform is the right call

If a standard platform fits your store 90% of the way and the fees are manageable, we will tell you to keep it. Custom makes sense when the fees, the workarounds or the limits start costing more than they save.
