Dispatch and coordination software for funeral services

Structured case intake and assignment, built around how your service actually works, so no call or handoff gets missed.

Funeral services do some of the most time-sensitive and personal work there is, and much of the coordination still happens by phone and text. We built Funeral Triage, a dispatch coordination app for a direct-to-families funeral service, to replace those ad-hoc handoffs with a structured system. It was built for and paid for by the client and is in active daily use.

What the software does

It organises incoming cases and coordinates assignment, so the team has one place to see what has come in and who is handling it, instead of relying on whoever answered the phone.

What else could help

The same approach extends naturally: intake forms that feed straight into the case list, scheduling, family-facing updates through a simple portal, and reporting for the owner. Every service is different, so we start with the one tool that would help most and grow from there. See what we build.
