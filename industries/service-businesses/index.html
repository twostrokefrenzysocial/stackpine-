Custom software for service businesses

Requests, bookings, dispatch and follow-up in one place, so nothing falls through the cracks between the phone, the texts and the inbox.

Service businesses live and die on response time. Requests arrive by phone, text, email and walk-in; jobs get handed off by text; and the owner ends up as the switchboard. The fix usually starts with one simple tool that catches every request in one place, and grows from there one layer at a time.

What we build for service businesses

  • Intake and triage so every request lands in one place, sorted and routed.
  • Scheduling and booking so customers book themselves and get confirmed automatically.
  • Dispatch and assignment to replace ad-hoc phone and text handoffs.
  • Customer portals to end the “any update?” calls.
  • Email automation and AI assistants that take the busywork off your desk.

Where to start

Follow the dropped balls. Requests that arrive by four different channels are the ones that go missing, so intake is often the highest-value first layer. After that, the same system can schedule, quote and report. Read how it works.

Common questions

What is the best first piece of software for a service business?
Usually intake: one place where every request lands, whatever channel it came in through. It is quick to build, and it makes every later layer (scheduling, quoting, reporting) easier.
Do I need to know anything about technology?
No. You describe how your business runs in plain English, and turning that into software is our job.
