Answered straight, no jargon

How long does a build take?
Most first versions are up and working in a few weeks, not months. Simple tools can take days. You see progress the whole way through, so there’s never a six-month black box between “we talked” and “here’s your software.”
Do I need to know anything about technology?
Not a thing. You describe how your business runs in plain English: “requests come in by text and we lose track of them” is a perfect starting point. Turning that into software is our job, not yours.
What does it cost?
Every project is quoted custom, because every business is different, but there’s no big upfront bill, and hosting, support, updates and improvements are all included in one predictable monthly fee. Most owners are pleasantly surprised.
What happens when I reach out?
A normal conversation, not a sales pitch. Tell us what’s slowing your business down, we’ll ask a few questions, and you’ll get an honest free quote. If we think you don’t actually need custom software, we’ll tell you.
