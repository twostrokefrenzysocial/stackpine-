Custom websites and online stores

A professional site that’s actually yours, kept fast, secure and fresh, for one small monthly fee.

Most small-business websites die one of two deaths: the DIY builder site that never quite looked professional, or the one-time agency site that was beautiful in year one and abandoned by year three. There’s a third option, and it’s how we build every site at Stackpine.

The problem with the usual two options

DIY website builders

Wix, Squarespace and their cousins are genuinely fine for a hobby page. For a business, the cracks show fast: your site looks like everyone else’s template, every hour you spend fighting the editor is an hour off your actual business, and the monthly fees quietly climb once you add the features you really need: bookings, payments, more pages, removing their branding.

One-time agency builds

An agency site starts strong, but it’s a snapshot. Nobody updates it, the plugins age, the “News” page still shows 2023, and when something breaks there’s an invoice for every fix. A stale site quietly tells customers a business isn’t paying attention.

How we do it instead

We build your site custom (designed around your business, not squeezed into a template) for a lower one-time build cost than a traditional agency. Then, for a small monthly fee, we take care of it like it’s ours:

  • Hosting and security: fast, encrypted and monitored, so you never think about servers
  • Updates and fixes: new hours, new photos, new pages, seasonal promos. Message us and it’s done, no invoice per change
  • Freshness: the site evolves with the business instead of aging in place
  • Search-engine care: proper titles, speed and structure so Google can actually find you

One relationship, no runaroundThe person who built your site is the person who maintains it. No support tickets, no “that’s a third-party plugin issue.”

Online stores, without the platform squeeze

If you sell products, the big e-commerce platforms want a monthly platform fee, app fees on top, and a cut of your transactions. That math works for them, not for you. A custom storefront gives you product pages, checkout and an admin panel simple enough for one person to run, with no per-app surcharges and no platform deciding what your store can do.

One of our recent builds replaced a big-platform store for an off-road parts retailer: full inventory, checkout and order management, run day-to-day by a single owner.

What about a website and software?

This is where custom really pays off. Because we build software too, your website doesn’t have to be a brochure. It can do things. Online booking that fills your calendar. A customer portal where clients check their order status instead of calling. A quote request form that lands in a real intake system instead of an inbox.

Start with the site; add working layers when you’re ready. That’s the idea behind our name. Read how custom software works when a website alone isn’t enough.
