# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: owners of small businesses (trades, retail, services, often in Ohio) who are not technical. Their work runs on spreadsheets, group texts and off-the-shelf apps that almost fit. They come to the site to find out whether custom software is realistic and affordable for them, and how to start a conversation. The Builds page is supporting proof for anyone who wants to see the technical depth; it is not the main audience.

## Product Purpose
Stackpine builds custom business software, business apps, websites and online stores shaped around how a particular business runs. Everything is delivered on one monthly subscription that covers the build, hosting, support and ongoing improvements. Success means a visitor understands the offer, believes it's for a business like theirs, and sends an enquiry through the contact form.

## Positioning
No large upfront bill: software is built "one layer at a time", starting narrow and growing, with the same person still there next month and next year. The plain-English, honest tone ("if you don't need custom software, we'll say so") is part of the offer.

## Operating Context
A static site (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step) hosted on GitHub Pages at stackpine.org. The enquiry form posts to the stackpine-admin backend, and a small self-hosted analytics beacon (assets/beacon.js) runs on every page. SEO matters: pages target real search queries, with JSON-LD, canonical URLs and a sitemap.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: home, how-it-works, what-we-build, pricing, results, builds, contact, plus guides (custom-software, websites, ohio).
- No published prices: every project is quoted individually.
- Results are anonymous by design.
- Keep the static, dependency-free stack.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Stackpine. Mark: a stacked-chevron pine tree (logo.png, cover.jpg).
- Brand colours from the logo and cover art: pine green and slate navy.
- Tagline in the cover art: "Building the Future, One Layer at a Time."
- Voice: plain English, friendly, no jargon, honest.

## Evidence on Hand
- Three anonymous results (healthcare leadership dashboard, off-road parts storefront, motorsports expense tracker), with stated outcomes.
- Seven technical builds on /builds/ with metrics.
- There are no testimonials, client logos or published prices. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Plain English first: a non-technical owner should never feel talked down to or lost.
2. Honesty over hype: every claim on the site must be one Stackpine can stand behind.
3. Start small, grow in layers: the site should make the first step feel small.
4. The enquiry is the goal: every page leads to the contact form.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG AA. Visitors include owners on phones between jobs, so mobile readability and touch targets matter.
