Common Questions

Answered straight, no jargon

How long does a build take?

Most first versions are up and working in a few weeks — not months. Simple tools can take days. You see progress the whole way through, so there's never a six-month black box between "we talked" and "here's your software."

Do I need to know anything about technology?

Not a thing. You describe how your business runs in plain English — "requests come in by text and we lose track of them" is a perfect starting point. Turning that into software is our job, not yours.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted custom, because every business is different — but there's no big upfront bill, and hosting, support, updates, and improvements are all included in one predictable monthly fee. Most owners are pleasantly surprised. Message us and we'll give you a straight number, free.

What happens when I reach out?

A normal conversation — not a sales pitch. Tell us what's slowing your business down, we'll ask a few questions, and you'll get an honest free quote. If we think you don't actually need custom software, we'll tell you that too.

What if I need changes later?

That's the whole point of how we work. Your subscription includes ongoing improvements — as your business changes, your software changes with it. No change-order invoices, no disappearing developer.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Everything is month to month, you can cancel anytime, and your data is always yours — if you ever leave, we'll hand over an export and part as friends.
