Custom online store

An off-road parts retailer wanted a real online store without platform fees eating the margin. It got one that a single owner runs day to day.

Full store + admin, live in weeksOff-road parts retailer. Client details are kept anonymous, because our clients’ operations are their business.

The problem

The business needed a real online store, but the big e-commerce platforms charge a monthly platform fee, app fees on top and a cut of transactions. For a parts retailer, those fees were eating the margin.

What we built

A custom storefront that replaced a big-platform store: product pages, full inventory, checkout and order management, with an admin panel simple enough for one person to run.

What changed

The full store and its admin panel were live in weeks, and the store is run day to day by a single owner, with no per-app surcharges and no platform deciding what the store can do.

Is this a fit for your business?

If you sell products and your platform bill keeps growing with every app and every sale, a custom storefront is worth pricing out. Read more about how our websites and online stores work.
