RevLane / OpenThrottle

A SaaS platform that recovers declined-service revenue for auto and powersports shops.

Production SaaS · verified by 56 testsRevenue recovery SaaS.

The problem

Auto and powersports shops lose revenue when customers decline recommended service or walk out on a quote, and nobody follows up.

What we built

A SaaS platform that tracks declined services and walk-out leads and runs automated SMS follow-up to recover the revenue. It includes shop dashboards, admin tooling (impersonation, a health-check watchdog, daily digests, trial and churn automation) and a performance-based pricing engine.

What changed

A production SaaS platform, verified by a 56-test production suite.

The stack

  • Node/Express
  • React
  • SQLite
  • Railway
  • Vercel
  • Telnyx 10DLC with Ed25519 webhook signature verification
  • Stripe
  • Resend

Is this a fit for your business?

The lesson travels well beyond shops: any business that quotes work and waits for a yes is leaving money on the table when nobody follows up. Automated, polite follow-up is a common layer we add to intake and quoting tools.
