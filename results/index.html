Why these are anonymous

Our clients’ operations are their business. How a healthcare organization staffs its programs, or what a retailer’s margins look like, isn’t ours to publish, and the same discretion would apply to your build. What we can describe is the shape of the problem and what measurably changed.

What “results” actually means here

We don’t count features shipped. The numbers worth reporting are the ones an owner feels: hours no longer spent compiling reports, requests that stop falling through, invoices that go out the same day instead of next week. Every figure on this page is a conservative estimate taken from the people doing the work before and after.

  • Time recovered: measured against how the task was done before, not against a theoretical best case.
  • Errors removed: duplicate entry, missed handoffs and version confusion usually disappear first.
  • Response time: how long a customer waits is often the metric that changes most visibly.
