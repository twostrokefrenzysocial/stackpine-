Resume Screening Pipeline

A privacy-first pipeline that scores resumes against a job description with a locally hosted LLM.

Zero PII leaves the buildingPrivacy-first candidate triage.

The problem

A large resume backlog plus a live stream of new applicants, with strict data privacy requirements.

What we built

The pipeline pulls resumes from Google Drive, extracts the text and scores each candidate against the job description on weighted dimensions (JD match, behavioral adaptability, AI and tech leverage) using a locally hosted LLM, so no personal information leaves the building. It writes ranked results with a rationale to Google Sheets, logs failures for review, and covers both a one-time backlog burn and an always-on watch folder.

It is framed as triage assistance with a human in the loop reviewing every result, never as a hiring decision-maker.

What changed

The backlog and the incoming stream are both triaged automatically, with zero PII leaving the building.

The stack

  • n8n
  • Google Drive
  • Google Sheets
  • Ollama on on-prem hardware

Is this a fit for your business?

Hiring is one of the most time-consuming jobs in a growing business. A tool that sorts the pile, explains its reasoning and leaves every decision to a person can save hours without handing judgment to a machine.
