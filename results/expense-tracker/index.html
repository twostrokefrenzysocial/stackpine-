Expense tracker

Receipts used to live in a shoebox until tax season. Now they are captured from a phone in seconds and stay sorted all year.

Tax prep: days → one tapMotorsports business. Client details are kept anonymous, because our clients’ operations are their business.

The problem

Receipts lived in a shoebox until tax season, which meant days of sorting and adding up at the worst possible time of year.

What we built

An expense tracker where every expense is snapped from a phone in seconds and sorted automatically, so the totals are always current.

What changed

Totals stay tax-ready all year long. Tax prep went from days to one tap, by the estimate of the people doing the work.

Is this a fit for your business?

Small tools like this are often the cheapest way to start with custom software: one job, done well, in use quickly. If there is a chore in your business that piles up until a deadline forces it, it is probably a good first layer.
