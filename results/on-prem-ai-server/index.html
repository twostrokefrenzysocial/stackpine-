On-Prem AI Server

An on-premises inference layer that keeps compliance-sensitive AI workloads entirely in the building.

Nemotron Super 49B running fully on-premNVIDIA DGX Spark.

The problem

Compliance-sensitive workflows, such as intake and case-adjacent processing, cannot run on cloud LLMs.

What we built

An on-premises inference layer that serves a large local model through an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. It powers local document RAG (answers grounded in the organization’s own documents), privacy-sensitive processing, and scheduled agent runs as persistent services.

What changed

AI tooling became usable for workflows that could not touch the cloud, with the model, the documents and the processing all staying on hardware in the building.

The stack

  • NVIDIA DGX Spark
  • Ollama
  • Nemotron Super 49B
  • systemd services

Is this a fit for your business?

Most small businesses will never need their own AI hardware. But if your data genuinely cannot leave the building, it is good to know that AI assistants are still on the table. Ask us whether it makes sense for you; often a simpler setup does the job.
