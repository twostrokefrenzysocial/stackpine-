Staff Knowledge Assistant

An internal AI assistant that answers staff policy and procedure questions from the organization’s own documents.

~30 hrs/week of interruptions absorbedInternal RAG chatbot.

The problem

Staff interrupted supervisors constantly for policy and procedure answers buried in documents.

What we built

An internal AI assistant grounded in the organization’s ingested documents (PDF, DOCX and TXT), with OCR for scanned files, streaming answers, voice input, a feedback loop and per-document re-indexing. When the documents do not cover a question, it says so honestly instead of guessing. It has since been upgraded from document lookup to an operational reasoning advisor.

What changed

It absorbs an estimated 30 hours a week of procedural questions and supervisor interruptions.

The stack

  • Node/Express
  • React/Vite PWA
  • SQLite FTS5
  • tesseract.js + pdfjs-dist OCR
  • SSE streaming
  • JWT auth
  • Railway
  • Automated daily Box backups

Is this a fit for your business?

If you are the person everyone asks, and the answers are already written down somewhere, an assistant like this gives you hours back every week. It works best for businesses with a handbook, procedures or a binder of policies new staff never quite finish reading.
