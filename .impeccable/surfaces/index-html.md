## Scope
Whole marketing site (home, sub-pages, guides). Visitor mode: Persuade. Code-led.

## Audience and job
Non-technical small-business owners, often on a phone between jobs. Job: understand the offer, believe it fits a business like theirs, start an enquiry.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The Stackpine mark is a pine made of stacked layers, and the product is built one layer at a time. Every section is one full-bleed colour field stacked on the next. This replaces the earlier Trailhead direction at the owner's request (taste-skill rerun). It rejects the centred headline over icon cards, and dark neon.
OWN-WORLD: Flat fields of map white, land, navy, deep pine and blaze blue, with no dividers between them. League Spartan 700 display, Figtree text. Square corners, one signpost button with an arrow point, no hairlines, gradients or resting shadows. Pictures are crops of Stackpine's cover art.
STORY: The visitor sees what Stackpine does in five words, reads the cost of "almost fits", opens the stages, sees ten real builds, learns it is $0 up front, and types what's slowing them down.
