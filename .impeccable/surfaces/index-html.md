## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is a trailhead. Custom software is a trail you walk one marker at a time, not a $40k cliff. It rejects the dark neon "tech studio" page and the centred-headline-over-icon-cards template.
OWN-WORLD: Deep pine-green routed sign boards (Committed: pine owns the hero and closing board) with pale routed lettering. Light map-white ground (#F5F8F4) with a land-tint band, logo-navy ink, and blue Buckeye Trail blazes (2:5 rectangles) as the only marker system. Rounded routed-sign corners on boards, square everywhere else. Overpass display face (highway-signage lineage) and Public Sans for text. No eyebrows, no icon tiles, no glow.
STORY: The visitor sees in plain words what Stackpine does, reads the destination board (how it works, cost, proof), believes the low-risk start, and types what's slowing them down.
